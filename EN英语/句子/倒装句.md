


### 部分倒装

部分倒装：否定词、绝对词、频度词、虚拟发生了提前
- 翻译时先将倒装部分换元，再加入汉语中表示强调的功能性词汇

Only gradually **was** the by-product of the institution **noted** , and only more gradually still **was** this effect **considered as** a directive factor *in* the conduct *of* the institution.
-  the by-product of the institution **was Only gradually noted** , and this effect **was still only more gradually considered as** a directive factor *in* the conduct *of* the institution.
- 这一制度的副产品仅仅逐渐才被注意到，并且其作用被视为这一制度运转的指导性因素，这一过程则是更为缓慢的


Beethoven's habit *of increasing* the volume *with* an extreme intensity and then abruptly following it with a sudden soft passage was only rarely used by composers before him .
