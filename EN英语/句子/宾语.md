## 结构

### 双宾语

`S make N1 N2` ==> `S make N1 (be) N2` 宾从

The emphasis *on* data *gathered* first-hand, *combined with* a cross-cultural perspective *brought  to* the analysis *of* cultures past and present , **makes** this study a unique and distinctly important social science.
- 对于被收集到一手数据的重视，结合了分析过去和现在的文化所产生的跨文化视角，正是这种重视使这项研究成为了一门独特并且十分重要的社会科学


By all accounts he was a freethinking person , and a courageous one , and I find courage an essential quality for the understanding, let alone the performance, of his works.
- 综上所述，贝多芬是一个思想不受约束的人，而且是一个勇敢的人，并且我发现勇气是对于理解更是演奏他作品的重要品质

### 宾补

`S make N1 补语` ==> `S make N1 (be) 补语` 宾从

- 强大的记忆力并没有让我的情感变得更敏锐或逼真

### 宾补倒装

S make 补语 N ==>S make N (be) 补

Thus , the anthropological concept of " culture ," *like* the concept *of* " set " in mathematics , is an abstract concept ==which== **makes** possible immense amounts *of* concrete research and understanding .
- 因此，文化的人类学概念就像在数学中集合的概念一样，是一种抽象概念，它使得大量具体的研究和理解称为可能

They **may teach** very well , and *more than* earn their salaries , but most *of* them **make** little or no independent reflections *on* human problems ==which== **involve** moral judgment .
- 但是他们中的大部分人，对涉及到人类道德判断能力做出的对立思考是很少或几乎没有的

### 形宾

`S make (it N2/补 + to do/从句)` 形宾 ==> `S make (to do/从句 be N2/补语)`
