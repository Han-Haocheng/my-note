形式主语：主语过长避免头重脚轻，与非限定性定语从句互为倒装形式
- 格式：S V O，which is adj.-> It **is** adj. ==that== S **V** O



On the north bank of the Ohio River sits Evansville Ind., home of David Williams, 52, and of a riverboat casino.
- 格式：Prep O Vi N 同位 ==> S Vi prep O.

Along with the many folks *looking* to make a permanent home *in* the United States came those ==who== **had** no intention to stay, 
- 格式：Prep O Vi N 并列定 ==> S Vi prep O.

## 翻译

### 形式主语的翻译

1. 将 it 翻译成泛指的“我们”、“人们”
2. 后面的单词根据中文的需要可以改变词性，但不变词义
3. 然后完全按照顺序翻译
