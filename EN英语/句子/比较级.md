# 比较

## 特点
- 比较级中名比动长可以做倒装
- 比较不同事物相同方面翻译成比较级
- sb.‘s n. adj. than adj. 比较相同主语不同方面，翻译成：与其说……不如说……
	- A+ more than B-
	- more A+ than B-
	- A- less than B+
	- less A- than B+
	- 与其说( - )不如说( + )

- `not so much ... as (but)...`<==>`might as well .. as` <==> `rather than` 与其说……不如说……


- not 否定比较级；no 否定形容词


### no more ... than

`no more adj./N. than` <==> `not V. any more than` 和……一样都不

In October the Department *of* Justice filed a brief in the Myriad case, arguing ==that== an isolated DNA molecule “is no less a product of nature... *than* **are** cotton fibres ==that== **have been separated** *from* cotton seeds.”


==If== appropriate public policies **were** *in* place *to* help all women - whether CEOs or their children’s caregivers -- and all families, Sandberg **would be** no more  newsworthy *than* any other highly capable person *living in* a more just society.

The heart **is** no more intelligent *than* the stomach, *for* they **are both controlled** *by* the brain.

A man cannot fly any more than a bird can speak.

## 性质

- I play basketball batter thank CXK（省略性：省略对称倒装）
- I play basketball better than CXK does（对称性）
- I play basketball better than does CXK（倒装性）

- 对称性和倒装性要翻译出时态的差异
- 描述数据差异和数据变换

## 翻译

- not than 不比
- no better than 一样坏
