# 四级范文

## 2025-1

### passage 1


As a fundamental discipline, College Chinese plays a crucial role in higher education. 

作为一门基础学科, 大学语文在高等教育中发挥着至关重要的作用。

I firmly support making it a compulsory course because it enhances students’ language proficiency, cultural literacy, and analytical abilities. 

我坚定支持将其设为必修课程,因为它能提升学生的语言能力、文化素养和分析能力。

### passage 2

First, College Chinese strengthens students’ reading comprehension and writing skills, which are indispensable for academic papers and professional reports.

首先,大学语文课程能强化学生的阅读理解和写作技能,这些技能对学术论文与专业报告的撰写不可或缺。

Many graduates struggle with formal documentation, and this course provides systematic training. 

许多毕业生在正式文书写作方面存在困难,而该课程能提供系统训练。


Second, through studying classical and contemporary Chinese works, students gain deeper cultural insights and develop national identity, which is particularly valuable in today’s globalized world.

其次,通过学习中国古今文学作品,学生能够深化文化认知并培养民族认同感,这在当今全球化时代尤为珍贵。

Most importantly, College Chinese cultivates sophisticated analytical skills.
最重要的是,大学语文能培养高阶分析能力。

By interpreting complex texts such as ancient poems and philosophical essays, students learn to identify implicit meanings, evaluate different perspectives, and construct well-reasoned arguments—abilities that go beyond academic boundaries and are highly applicable to careers in law, business, and medicine.

通过解读古诗文和哲学典籍等复杂文本,学生能学会领会深层含义、权衡多元观点并构建缠密论证,这些超越学术疆界的素养在法律、商业及医学等职业领域都具有极高的实用价值。


### passage 3

In conclusion, mandating College Chinese ensures students acquire essential communication skills, cultural literacy, and analytical thinking capabilities. 

综上所述,将大学语文设为必修课能确保学生掌握必要的沟通技能、文化素养与思辨能力。

These competencies make graduates more competitive in the job market while preserving China’s literary heritage. 

这些素养既能增强毕业生的就业竞争力,又能传承中华文脉。

Universities should therefore recognize its comprehensive benefits.

因此,高校应当认识到其综合价值。
