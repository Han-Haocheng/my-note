## 1 Passage

A new survey by Harvard University finds more than two-thirds of young Americans disapprove of President Trump's use of Twitter.

哈佛大学的一项新调查发现，超过三分之二的美国年轻人不赞成特朗普总统使用推特。

The implication is that Millennials prefer news from the White House to be filtered through other source, Not a president's social media platform.

言外之意，千禧一代更喜欢通过其他渠道，而不是总统的社交媒体平台，来过滤来自白宫的新闻。

## 2 Passage

Most Americans rely on social media to check daily headlines.

大多数美国人依赖社交媒体查看每日头条新闻。

Yet as distrust has risen toward all media, people may be starting to beef up their media literacy skills.
然而，随着对所有媒体的不信任感上升，人们可能开始增强他们的媒体素养。

Such a trend is badly needed.
这种趋势是迫切需要的。

During the 2016 presidential campaign, nearly a quarter of web content shared by Twitter users in the politically critical state of Michigan was fake news, according to the University of Oxford.

据牛津大学（University of Oxford）统计，在 2016 年总统竞选期间，政治敏感的密歇根州 Twitter 用户分享的网络内容中，近四分之一是假新闻。

And a survey conducted for Buzz Feed News found 44 percent of Facebook users rarely or never trust news from the media giant.

而一项针对 BuzzFeed 新闻的调查发现，44%的 Facebook 用户很少或从不信任这家媒体巨头的新闻。

## 3 Passage

Young people who are digital natives are indeed becoming more skillful at separating fact from fiction in cyberspace. A Knight Foundation focus-group survey of young people between ages 14 and 24 found they use “distributed trust” to verify stories. They cross-check sources and prefer news from different perspectives—especially those that are open about any bias. “Many young people assume a great deal of personal responsibility for educating themselves and actively seeking out opposing viewpoints,” the survey concluded.

在数字时代出生的年轻人确实越来越善于在网络空间里把事实和小说区分开来。奈特基金会焦点小组对 14 至 24 岁年轻人的调查发现，他们使用“分布式信任”来验证故事。他们反复核对消息来源，喜欢从不同角度看新闻，尤其是那些对任何偏见持开放态度的新闻。调查的结论是：“许多年轻人在自我教育和积极寻求对立观点方面承担着很大的个人责任。”。

## 4 Passage

Such active research can have another effect. A 2014 survey conducted in Australia, Britain, and the United States by the University of Wisconsin-Madison found that young people's reliance on social media led to greater political engagement.


这种积极的研究还有另一个效果。威斯康星大学麦迪逊分校（University of Wisconsin Madison）2014 年在澳大利亚、英国和美国进行的一项调查发现，年轻人对社交媒体的依赖导致了更多的政治参与。

## 5 Passage

Social media allows users to experience news events more intimately and immediately while also permitting them to re-share news as a projection of their values and interests. This forces users to be more conscious of their role in passing along information. A survey by Barna research group found the top reason given by Americans for the fake news phenomenon is “reader error,” more so than made-up stories or factual mistakes in reporting. About a third say the problem of fake news lies in “misinterpretation or exaggeration of actual news” via social media．In other words, the choice to share news on social media may be the heart of the issue. “This indicates there is a real personal responsibility in counteracting this problem,” says Roxanne Stone, editor in chief at Barna Group.


社交媒体允许用户更直接、更直接地体验新闻事件，同时也允许他们重新分享新闻，以此作为他们价值观和兴趣的投影。这迫使用户更加意识到他们在传递信息方面的作用。巴纳研究机构（Barna research group）的一项调查发现，美国人对假新闻现象给出的首要原因是“读者错误”，而不是捏造故事或报道中的事实错误。大约三分之一的人认为，虚假新闻的问题在于通过社交媒体“曲解或夸大真实新闻”，换句话说，选择在社交媒体上分享新闻可能是问题的核心。巴纳集团（BarnaGroup）总编辑罗克珊•斯通（Roxanne Stone）表示：“这表明，在应对这一问题上，个人确实负有责任。”。

## 6 Passage

So when young people are critical of an over-tweeting president, they reveal a mental discipline in thinking skills – and in their choices on when to share on social media.


因此，当年轻人对一位过度发推特的总统提出批评时，他们会在思维能力上显示出一种精神上的约束，也会在他们选择何时在社交媒体上分享信息方面有所体现。
