# 向量组

**向量组**：若干个同尾数的列[[向量|向量]]（行向量）所组成的集合

- 若 $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\dots,\alpha_{r},\dots,\alpha_{s}$，其中 $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\dots,\alpha_{r}$ 是 $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\dots,,\alpha_{s}$ *部分组*，$\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\dots,\alpha_{s}$ 是*整体组*
